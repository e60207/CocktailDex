The `#Original` tag is reserved for cocktails invented by the owner (Eric). It must never be applied to cocktails created by other people, even if they are undocumented originals.

**Why:** Eric corrected this after The Lower Wacker was mistakenly tagged `#Original` — it's an Anders Erickson original, so it got `#AndersErickson` instead.

**How to apply:** When ingesting a cocktail created by a known third-party creator, add a creator-specific tag (e.g. `#AndersErickson`) to the VOCABULARY under Theme/Era and use that instead of `#Original`. Only use `#Original` when the owner explicitly claims authorship.
