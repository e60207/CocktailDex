## {The Belmont Jewel}

**Theme/era:** TBD

**Background:** TBD

- **Glassware:** Rock
- **Ingredients:**
  - 1.5 oz Bourbon whiskey
  - 3/4 oz fresh lemon juice 
  - 1/2 oz grenadine
  - 1/2 oz Lustau Oloroso Don Nuño Sherry 
  - 2-3 oz sparkling Water 
- **Instruction:** Add all ingredients except for sparkling water into shaking tin. Add ice and shake. Add ice to chilled glass, simultaneously pour the mixed drink and sparkling water into glass. Garnish with mint sprigs.
