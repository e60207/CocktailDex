## {French 75}

**Background:** TBD

- **Glassware:** flute
- **Ingredients:**
  - 2 oz Brut sparkling wine 
  - 1 oz  Gin OR Cognac 
  - 1/2 oz fresh lemon juice 
  - 1/2 oz simple syrup 
- **Instruction:** Pour approx. 2oz of sparkling wine into Champaign flute. Add all remaining ingredients and ice into shaking tin. Shake and strain into the glass. Top up with more sparkling wine and garnish with a lemon twist.
- **Garnish:** Lemon twist
- **Profile:** Bubbly, crisp, and refreshing.
