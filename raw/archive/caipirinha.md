## {Caipirinha}

**Theme/era:** TBD

**Background:** TBD

- **Glassware:** Rock
- **Ingredients:**
  - 1/2 lime (cut into slices) 
  - 1 tbsp. cane sugar 
  - 2 oz Cachaça (Barrel aged)
- **Instruction:** Add sliced lime and sugar into glass. Muddle well. Add the spirit and crush ice then briefly stir to mix. Garnish with a lime wage and serve with a straw. 
- **Garnish:** Lime wage
