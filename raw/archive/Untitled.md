## {Sazerac}

**Theme/era:** TBD

**Background:** TBD

- **Glassware:** Small Rock/Espresso glass.
- **Ingredients:**
  - 1.5 oz Sazerac Straight Rye Whiskey 
  - 1/2 oz Maison Rouge VSOP Cognac 
  - 1/4 oz semi-rich simple syrup 
  - 3 dashes Peychaud's Bitters 
  - 1 dash Angostura Aromatic Bitters 
  - Rinse of Absinthe 
- **Instruction:** Add all ingredients into mixing glass and stir to chill. Rinse the chilled glass with Absinthe. Then pour drink into glass. Express oil from lemon peal and lay it across the glass.
