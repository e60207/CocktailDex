## {Whiskey Sour}

**Background:** TBD

- **Glassware:** Sour glass
- **Ingredients:**
  - 1.5 oz Bourbon whiskey
  - 3/4 oz lemon juice
  - 1/2 oz  rich Demerara syrup
  - 1/2 oz raw egg white OR aquafaba (chickpea water)
  - 3 drops Angostura Aromatic Bitters and lemon oil for garnish  
- **Instruction:** Add all ingredients (except angostura bitter and lemon oil) into shaking tin, dry shake vigorously to create foamy texture. Add ice and shake to chill the drink. Double strain the cocktail into chilled glass. Add 3 drops of angostura bitter and connect the dot with a cherry pick to make a heart shape. Express lemon oil. 
- **Garnish:** Angostura bitters and lemon oil
- **Profile:** Sweet and sour. Silky foamy texture.
