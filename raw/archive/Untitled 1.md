## {Bloody Caesar}

**Theme/era:** TBD

**Background:** TBD

- **Glassware:** Collins
- **Ingredients:**
  - 1.5 oz Vodka 
  - 3 oz Mott's Clamato Juice 
  - 2 dashes Worcestershire Sauce 
  - 2 dashes Tabasco Sauce 
  - Juice of 1/4 lime 
- **Instruction:** Prepare the glass rim with lime, squeeze in the lime juice. Add ice then add all ingredients into chilled glass. Stir to mix well, garnish with Celery stalk.
- **Garnish:** Celery salt rimmed glass and Celery stalk.
