# The Lower Wacker

**Background:** An original cocktail by bartender and content creator Anders Erickson, The Lower Wacker takes its name from the lower level of Wacker Drive in Chicago — the subterranean service road beneath the city's iconic two-tiered boulevard, known for its utilitarian grit and as a symbol of Chicago's hidden underbelly. The drink leans into that identity: rye whiskey's peppery backbone is tempered by the artichoke bitterness of Cynar and the sweetness of vermouth and apricot, then the whole thing is ringed with Jeppson's Malört, a notoriously bitter wormwood-based spirit that has been a Chicago rite of passage since the 1930s. (source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bzN1zwAqgSY, 6:48–10:12)

![](../photos/_TBD/pending-cocktails.jpg)

- **Glassware:** Coupe
- **Ingredients:**
  - 2 oz Rye Whiskey
  - 1/2 oz Sweet Vermouth
  - 1/2 oz Cynar
  - 1/4 oz Apricot Liqueur
  - 1 dash Angostura bitters
  - Jeppson's Malört rinse
- **Instruction:** Add all ingredients into a mixing glass. Stir to chill. Rinse a chilled coupe with Jeppson's Malört, discarding the excess, then strain the mixed drink into the glass. Express lemon oil over the surface and add a cocktail cherry.
- **Garnish:** Cocktail cherry.
- **Profile:** Bold and unabashedly bitter — rye's peppery warmth meets the artichoke-forward bitterness of Cynar, softened by sweet vermouth and a touch of apricot, with a bracing Malört rinse that is quintessentially Chicago.
