# Rabo de Galo

**Background:** Rabo de Galo (Portuguese for "rooster's tail," a play on the word "cocktail") is a classic Brazilian stirred drink built on cachaça, sweet vermouth, and Cynar, an Italian artichoke-based amaro. It belongs to the same spirit-forward, low-dilution family as the Negroni and Manhattan, and has been a staple of São Paulo's bar scene for decades. The pairing of cachaça's grassy funk with Cynar's vegetal bitterness gives it a distinctly South American character. (source: https://www.diffordsguide.com/cocktails/recipe/1740/rabo-de-galo)

![](../photos/_TBD/pending-cocktails.jpg)

- **Glassware:** Rocks
- **Ingredients:**
  - 1.5 oz Cachaça
  - 0.5 oz Sweet Vermouth
  - 0.25 oz Cynar
- **Instruction:** Add all ingredients into a mixing glass with ice and stir until chilled. Strain into a chilled rocks glass. Express lime oil from the peel over the drink, then drop the peel into the glass as garnish.
- **Garnish:** Lime peel (expressed)
- **Profile:** Bittersweet and spirit-forward with earthy, vegetal notes from the Cynar, herbaceous depth from the vermouth, and grassy brightness from the cachaça.
