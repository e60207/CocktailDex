# Pago Pago

**Background:** The Pago Pago is a classic Daiquiri variation dating to circa 1940, credited to the Ronrico rum brand. It first appeared in the second edition of *The How and When* by Hyman Gale and Gerald F. Marco, published in Chicago that year. Named after Pago Pago, the capital of American Samoa (an American territory since 1900), the drink is notable for its unusual combination of tropical pineapple with the alpine herbal punch of Green Chartreuse and White Crème de Cacao — a Tiki-adjacent riff on the Daiquiri template. (source: https://punchdrink.com/articles/pago-pago-echo-lake-chartreuse-daiquiri-cocktail-recipe/)

![](../photos/_TBD/pending-cocktails.jpg)

- **Glassware:** Coupe
- **Ingredients:**
  - 3 pineapple cubes
  - 1.5 oz Rhum Agricole
  - 1/4 oz Green Chartreuse
  - 1/2 oz White Crème de Cacao
  - 1/2 oz fresh lime juice
- **Instruction:** Muddle pineapple in the shaking tin, then add all ingredients and ice. Shake and double strain into chilled glass. Garnish with pineapple frond.
- **Garnish:** Pineapple frond
- **Profile:** Tropical and complex — ripe pineapple up front, backed by the herbal intensity of Chartreuse, a whisper of chocolate from the crème de cacao, and a bright lime backbone.
