# Chicago Fizz

**Background:** The Chicago Fizz is a late-19th/early-20th-century classic whose exact Chicago origins remain hazy, but it was well-traveled enough to earn a mention in barman Albert S. Crockett's *The Old Waldorf Astoria Bar Book* (1935), where it was described as "an importation from the Windy City long before bombs, machine guns and sawed-off shotguns had come to disturb its peaceful life." The pairing of Jamaican rum with ruby port is unusual among fizzes, giving the drink a distinctive pinkish hue and a wine-inflected depth that sets it apart from simpler citrus fizzes of its era. (source: https://www.diffordsguide.com/cocktails/recipe/5860/chicago-fizz)

![](../photos/_TBD/pending-cocktails.jpg)

- **Glassware:** Collins
- **Ingredients:**
  - 1 oz Jamaican Rum
  - 1 oz Ruby Port
  - 1 oz lemon juice
  - 3/4 oz semi-rich simple syrup
  - 1 small egg white
  - 1 oz soda water
- **Instruction:** Add all ingredients to the shaking tin except for soda water. Dry shake vigorously without ice for 10–20 seconds. Add ice and shake to chill. Double strain into chilled Collins glass. Top with club soda and express lemon oil.
- **Garnish:** Straw.
- **Profile:** A sweet-tart pink fizz — Jamaican rum's molasses depth and ruby port's fruit-forward richness are brightened by lemon, then lifted into a frothy, effervescent sipper by the egg white and soda.
