# Caipirinha

**Background:** The Caipirinha is Brazil's national cocktail, officially designated as a cultural heritage item in 2003. Believed to have originated in the São Paulo countryside (particularly Piracicaba) in the early 20th century, it was initially used as a medicinal remedy for the Spanish flu. The name translates roughly to "little countryside drink," celebrating its rustic origins. It showcases cachaça, a Brazilian sugarcane spirit, muddled directly with lime and sugar over ice. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caipirinha)

![](../photos/_TBD/pending-cocktails.jpg)

- **Glassware:** Rocks
- **Ingredients:**
  - 1/2 Lime (cut into slices)
  - 1 tbsp. Cane sugar
  - 2 oz Cachaça (barrel aged)
- **Instruction:** Add sliced lime and sugar into glass. Muddle well. Add the spirit and crushed ice, then briefly stir to mix. Garnish with a lime wedge and serve with a straw.
- **Garnish:** Lime wedge
- **Profile:** Sweet, sour, and refreshing
